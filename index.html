01 — About

Production-first engineering for the telecom core.

I work on the operations side of telecom deployments — the part where a change either lands cleanly or takes a subscriber service down with it. Supporting Rakuten Mobile through Tech Mahindra, I handle day-to-day core network operations across Nokia and Mavenir functions, execute approved Standard, Normal and Emergency changes, and confirm the network is genuinely healthy afterwards.

My focus is SDM: HSS/HLR, UDM, AUSF, SDL, EIR and One NDS — plus the surrounding IMS/EPC fabric of SBC, DRA, STP, P-CSCF, S-CSCF, I-CSCF, PGW, SGW, PCRF, DNS, MRF, MGCF, BGCF, MSS and TAS. When something degrades, I go to the evidence: SIP, Diameter, GTP and S1AP traces, transaction logs, packet captures and CDRs — then hand L2/L3 and vendors a clear, reproducible finding.

What I bring to a deployment team: change discipline, pre/post validation that actually checks service (not just pod status), and documentation clean enough for the next shift to act on.
